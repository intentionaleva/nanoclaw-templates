# Sources

Use these, in this order, when you need a quantity, a rule, or a hazard. Search the web for the current page rather than quoting from memory; these pages change.

## National civil protection (first choice)

| Country | Body | Good for |
|---------|------|----------|
| Germany | BBK, bbk.bund.de/vorsorge | Water (2 L/person/day, 0.5 L of it for cooking), 10-day target with 3 days as the first step, checklists (food, water, Hausapotheke, Notgepäck, hygiene, fire, light and heat), siren meanings, NINA app, shelter rules ("2 walls"), cooking and heating without power |
| Germany | ernaehrungsvorsorge.de/private-vorsorge/notvorrat/vorratskalkulator | Food calculator by person and days |
| Germany | DWD (dwd.de), state Hochwassergefahrenkarten, LANUV/LfU | Weather and flood hazard maps |
| US | ready.gov/plan, ready.gov/kit, ready.gov/alerts | 4-step plan, family emergency communication plan and wallet card, kit list (1 gallon/person/day), FEMA app, Wireless Emergency Alerts |
| US | ready.gov/disability, ready.gov/seniors, ready.gov/kids, ready.gov/pets | Specific-needs planning |
| US | FEMA flood map service center, state wildfire maps, USGS earthquake hazard | Hazard maps |
| UK | gov.uk/prepare | Household guidance |
| Austria | Zivilschutzverband, siz.cc | Checklists |
| Switzerland | alertswiss.ch, BABS | Alerts and checklists |
| Spain | proteccioncivil.es, 112 | Alerts and guidance |
| Elsewhere | Search "<country> civil protection household emergency guidance" and the national Red Cross / Red Crescent page |

## Second choice

National Red Cross / Red Crescent societies (first aid, kit lists, courses). Municipal emergency-management pages (shelters, meeting points, sirens, local alert channels).

## Never a source

Commercial prepping shops, affiliate review sites, forums, social media. You may use a retailer page to check that a category of product exists in the household's country, never for a hazard, a quantity, or a safety rule.

## How to cite in prep/risks.md and in chat

Name the body, the page, and the date you checked. "Not in the HQ100 zone on the NRW Hochwassergefahrenkarte, checked 23 Oct 2026." If the map didn't load or the authority has no map for that hazard, write "could not verify" and say so to the household.
