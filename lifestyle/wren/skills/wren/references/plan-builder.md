# Plan builder

Input: `prep/profile.md` and `prep/risks.md`. Output: `prep/plan.md` in the format from `additional_context/plan-format.md`, and a first message with the reds.

## Tiering rules

**Red (this week).** Life safety and the things you cannot buy later. Every red item is €0 or under €20, takes under an hour, and would matter in the first 24 hours of any of the moderate/high hazards. Cap at 5. Order them by impact. The three universal reds, always first:

1. Official alert app installed and set to the home location (and school/work locations). For Germany: NINA. For the US: FEMA app plus Wireless Emergency Alerts on. Elsewhere: the national app from the risk pass.
2. Communication card: out-of-area contact chosen and told, two meeting points agreed, card drafted (`references/communication-plan.md`).
3. Three days of drinking water for everyone, pets included. Tap water in clean bottles counts.

Then, depending on profile and risks: 10-day medication reserve requested from the doctor (if anyone depends on medication); photos of ID, insurance and prescriptions saved to the phone and one cloud; smoke alarm test; where the kit lives agreed and told to everyone; one torch with working batteries where you can find it in the dark.

**Amber (next 4 to 8 weeks).** The standing stock and the kit, sized to the goal. Spread across the weeks by cost so no single week exceeds the monthly budget. Typical amber: water to goal (10 days = 2 L × people × 10 in Germany), food to goal, Hausapotheke to the BBK list, go-bag per person, hygiene and sanitation (bin liners, camping toilet if no cellar), power bank(s), battery or crank radio, cash reserve in small notes, carbon-monoxide detector if any combustion device is in the plan, fire extinguisher or spray, water container for flushing and washing, printed contact list, offline maps downloaded, the first drill.

**Green (3 to 12 months).** Resilience that costs real money or needs a decision: alternative cooking (camping stove rated for the use you'll put it to), alternative heating rated for indoor use, a power station or generator sized to the medical devices and the fridge, solar panel for the power station, satellite messenger or satellite internet if the risk pass found long outages or remote location, water filter, a first-aid course for two adults, structural work (shutters for heat, a safe interior room with two walls for the risks that call for it), a second kit in the car.

Goal "basics" stops at amber with 3-day quantities. Goal "solid" fills amber to 10 days and adds the first green items. Goal "max" fills everything and sizes power and comms to run without the grid for a month.

## Sizing

Compute from the profile and show it:

- Water: Germany/EU 2 L per person per day (0.5 L of that for cooking); US 1 gallon (3.8 L) per person per day including sanitation. Pets: dogs and cats roughly 50 ml per kg body weight per day; add it.
- Food: use the household's own eating habits; non-perishable, ready-to-eat or short cook time; infants and toddlers get their own line (formula, pouches, nappies). The BBK calculator at ernaehrungsvorsorge.de gives a worked list by person-days; use it for "solid" and "max".
- Medication: 10 days per person on daily medication; insulin and anything needing cooling gets a cooling plan in amber.
- Cash: enough for 3 days of groceries and fuel in small notes, in a fixed place.
- Power: list every device that must run (CPAP, oxygen concentrator, fridge for insulin, phone) with its wattage; that number sizes the green power item. Don't recommend a product; recommend a capacity.

## Timeline

Red: due dates within 7 days. Amber: due dates spread so weekly cost ≤ monthly budget ÷ 4 and no week has more than 3 items. Green: a target month, not a date, unless the household wants one.

If the budget can't cover amber within 8 weeks, extend amber and say so. If the budget is €0, amber becomes the free versions (tap water in cleaned bottles, food bought one extra item per shop, documents photographed, contact list handwritten) and the bought items move to green with a "when there's room" note.

## The first message

Send the reds as a numbered list with the "why" in half a sentence and the exact quantity where there is one. Then one line on amber ("Over the next six weeks we'll build the 10-day stock and the kits; about €40 a week at your budget.") and one line on green ("The power station and the first-aid course are the bigger ones; we'll talk about those in a few months."). End with the check-in day. Don't paste the full plan into chat; say it's in `prep/plan.md` and offer it.

## Updating

When an item is done, move it to Done with the date. When a profile fact changes, re-size the affected items only and say which ones moved. When the quarterly review re-runs risks, re-tier anything the new risks touch.
