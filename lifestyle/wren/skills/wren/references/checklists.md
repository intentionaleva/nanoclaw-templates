# Checklists

Master lists, drawn from the BBK brochure (11/2025, 2nd edition) and ready.gov/kit. Size them from `prep/profile.md`. Present only the slice that belongs to the current tier and week.

## Water

- Germany/EU: 2 L per person per day, of which 0.5 L for cooking. 3 days / 5 / 7 / 10 days are the BBK steps. Three people for 10 days is 60 L.
- US: 1 gallon (3.8 L) per person per day for drinking and sanitation.
- Pets: add their daily need.
- Separate container of non-drinking water for flushing and washing.
- Rotate bottled water by its date; tap water in cleaned containers every 6 months.

## Food

Criteria: keeps without a fridge, edible without cooking, or short cook time. Match the household's habits and allergies. Infants, toddlers and pets each get their own line. For "solid" and "max", point to the BBK calculator (ernaehrungsvorsorge.de) for a worked list and mark rotation dates.

## Hausapotheke (BBK list)

Personal medication (10-day reserve), painkillers, fever reducers, thermometer, anti-diarrhoea and anti-nausea, cold remedies, electrolytes, wound disinfectant, skin disinfectant, plasters and dressings, burn dressing, disposable gloves, cold compress, sunburn and insect-bite treatment, cooling gel for sprains, decongestant nasal spray, tweezers, scissors, wound and burn ointment.

## Light and heat

Torch and spare batteries; candles and lighter; warm clothing and blankets; sleeping bags; a heater that does not need mains power and is rated for indoor use; fuel for it, stored to the manufacturer's rule. Indoor combustion needs a carbon-monoxide alarm.

## Information and communication

Battery, solar or crank radio with the local frequency written on it; alert app installed; charged power bank; printed list of important numbers; offline maps.

## Fire

Smoke alarms in every room except kitchen and bathroom; fire extinguisher or extinguishing spray (class F for fat fires); carbon-monoxide alarm where anything burns indoors.

## Hygiene

Toothpaste and brushes, soap, personal hygiene items (period products, nappies), strong bin liners including ones that fit the toilet bowl, toilet paper, disinfectant, detergent, wet wipes, kitchen roll, rubber gloves, camping toilet.

## Go-bag (Notgepäck), one per person

Warm clothing, rain protection, sturdy shoes; change of clothes; first-aid material; personal medication; charged power bank; hygiene items; non-perishable food and a refillable bottle; folder with important documents (or the copies); sleeping bag or blanket; cup and cutlery; pocket knife and tin opener; torch; radio; lighter or matches; sun cream and hat; notepad and pen; work gloves; spares for glasses or hearing aids; cash. For children: comfort item, formula or snacks, nappies. For pets: carrier, lead, food for 3 days, vaccination record.

Keep keys, phone and wallet in a fixed place by the door.

## Documents (BBK "Dokumentenmappe")

Identity (birth certificate, ID, passport), ownership (land register, purchase contracts), financial claims (insurance policies), qualifications (certificates), rights (powers of attorney, court decisions), personal (wills, medical findings, vaccination records, password list), and a few photos you'd hate to lose. Originals or certified copies in a fire- and water-resistant container; digital copies in a cloud and on a stick. Check with the issuer whether an original is required.

## Other

Water container for firefighting or washing; respiratory mask; hard hat where the risk pass found debris hazards; cash beyond the go-bag.

## Pet kit

Food and water for the goal period, carrier, lead, medication, vaccination record, a photo of the pet, litter or bags.

## Kit storage

Home kit in a known place everyone can name. A smaller version at work and in the car. Check expiry dates at the monthly review; re-think the whole kit once a year and when the household changes.
