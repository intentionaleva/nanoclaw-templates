# Drills and alerts

## What a warning means

Germany (BBK): a one-minute rising-and-falling siren means danger, get information (radio, NINA, local channels). A one-minute steady tone is the all-clear. Warnings also come by app, Cell Broadcast to phones, radio, loudspeaker vans and digital boards. During a power or network outage many channels fail; the battery radio is the fallback.

US (ready.gov/alerts): Wireless Emergency Alerts on the phone, NOAA Weather Radio, the Emergency Alert System on radio and TV, and local opt-in systems. Check the phone's emergency alert settings are on.

Elsewhere: use what the risk pass found for the country.

## When a warning arrives

Say, in this order: 1. the official instruction for that warning if it's known (shelter, evacuate, stay away from windows, boil water); 2. switch on the radio or open the app for the local instruction; 3. text the out-of-area contact; 4. follow the plan's shelter rule for the hazard. Then stop advising and tell them to follow the authority's instructions. If anyone is hurt, the emergency number first.

## Shelter rules (BBK "Schutz suchen")

- Gas or vapour outside: go indoors, upper floors, close windows and doors, seal gaps, switch off ventilation. Most gases are heavier than air.
- Gas, vapour or hazardous liquid inside the building: leave the building.
- Radioactive release or explosion: as low as possible, away from windows and glass; interior rooms, cellars, underground car parks, underground stations. Thick walls and earth help.
- Flooding, heavy rain, flash or storm surge: higher floors. Never the cellar; a flooded cellar door can't be opened from inside and there's an electrocution risk. Avoid underground car parks, underground stations, underpasses.
- Explosions (also air raids): the "two walls" rule, two walls between you and the outside, no windows, not the top floor. Windowless cellar rooms are best; otherwise stairwells, bathrooms, corridors. Public buildings, underground car parks and stations work; stay far from entrances and windows, don't use lifts.
- Gas or vapour outside with no building in reach: the car, with ventilation and air conditioning off.

## Fire (BBK "Reagieren, wenn es brennt")

Only fight the fire if you're not in danger. Leave the room, close the door and windows behind you. Warn and help people in the household. Leave the building; never through smoke-filled rooms or stairwells, a few breaths of smoke can kill. If the escape route is smoky, make yourself heard at a window. Call the fire brigade. Warn the neighbours. Never water on burning fat, wax, oil or fuel; class F extinguisher or spray only.

## Drills

One per quarter, ten minutes, pick the one that matches the top risk:

1. **Text drill.** Everyone texts the out-of-area contact "OK at <place>" from wherever they are. Count who got through.
2. **Meeting-place drill.** Each person names the three meeting places from memory. Walk to the first one.
3. **Lights-out drill.** Switch off the mains for 30 minutes in the evening. Find the torch, the radio, the water; make a hot drink without the hob. Note what was missing.
4. **Go-bag drill.** Everyone picks up their bag and is at the door in five minutes. Note what's missing or too heavy.
5. **Shelter drill.** For the hazard that calls for it, go to the agreed interior room and check it holds everyone, including pets.
6. **Fire drill.** Escape route from each bedroom, where the extinguisher is, who calls.

After the drill, log what was missing as new plan items.
