# Risk assessment

One pass per location in `prep/profile.md`. Home gets the full pass; work, school and regular places get the short pass; trips get the travel pass. Write everything to `prep/risks.md` and show the household the evidence in chat, one line per hazard with the source.

Use web search. Prefer the official sources in `additional_context/sources.md`. Never state a hazard you couldn't find a source for; write "could not verify" instead.

## Full pass (home)

Check each of these and record likelihood (low / moderate / high), one line of evidence, the source, and the date:

1. **Flooding**: is the postcode in an official flood-hazard zone (HQ100 / HQextrem in Germany, FEMA flood zone in the US, the national equivalent elsewhere)? Also flash-flood exposure: steep terrain, small streams, recent events.
2. **Storm and wind**: regional storm history; for a house, exposed roof or trees nearby.
3. **Heat**: regional heatwave frequency; top-floor flat without shutters is higher.
4. **Winter**: snow and ice history, heating type (district heating and heat pumps stop when the power stops; gas boilers usually need power too).
5. **Power cuts**: rural grid, recent long outages reported by the grid operator, dependence on power for heating, water (own well?), medical devices.
6. **Water supply**: municipal or own well; any recent boil-water notices.
7. **Wildfire**: only where the region has wildfire risk (southern Europe, western US, parts of Australia); check the official map.
8. **Earthquake**: official seismic hazard zone.
9. **Industrial and nuclear**: Seveso / Störfall sites and nuclear plants within the official planning radius; the official emergency-planning zone pages list them. Also major rail or road hazmat routes if the authority flags them.
10. **Local alert channels**: the official app (NINA or KATWARN for Germany, FEMA app and Wireless Emergency Alerts in the US), siren system in the municipality, local radio frequency, municipal Notfalltreffpunkt / emergency meeting points if published.

11. **Nearby help** (only with the `osm` server): nearest hospital, pharmacy, fire station, police, shelter, with foot distance. See `references/nearby.md`. Record under a "Nearby help" heading in `prep/risks.md`.

Record the emergency numbers and the alert app in the profile too.

## Short pass (work, school, daycare, relatives)

Flood zone, industrial sites in range, the building's own emergency policy if public (schools and daycare centres usually publish a pickup policy), and the distance and route home without a car.

## Travel pass

For each trip: destination hazards in that season (hurricane season, wildfire season, heat, volcanic activity, earthquake), the local emergency number, the embassy or consulate, whether the country has an official alert app, and the one-line "if something happens: meeting point at the accommodation, out-of-area contact gets a text". Add to `prep/risks.md` under a travel heading and offer a one-page travel card two weeks before departure.

## How to present it

In chat, one line per hazard that is moderate or high, with the source, followed by the lows in one sentence. Say what you could not verify. No hazard gets a paragraph. Then: "Here's what that means for your plan" and go to the plan builder.

Example:

> Lindlar, 51789. Not in a mapped flood zone (NRW Hochwassergefahrenkarte, checked 23 Oct). Winter storms and multi-day power cuts are the realistic ones here: rural grid, heat pump, no cellar (grid operator outage reports; DWD). No Störfall site within the planning radius (NRW Umweltportal). Heat: moderate, top floor. Couldn't verify: local siren coverage, I'll check the Gemeinde page again at the next review.

## Re-checking

Re-run the home pass at every quarterly review and whenever a location changes. Update the check date.
