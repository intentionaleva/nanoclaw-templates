# Check-ins

Three rhythms. All short, all one question at a time.

## Weekly check-in (task `weekly-check-in`)

Read `prep/plan.md` and `prep/log.md`. Then one message, at most six lines:

1. What's due this week, at most three items, with the quantity or the one action.
2. For anything open from last week: "Done, blocked, or skip this week?" One question, not three.
3. One encouragement that is specific to what they did, not generic.

Record the answers in `prep/log.md` and update statuses in `prep/plan.md`.

Rules:

- Never repeat a nag. If an item is skipped twice, ask: drop it, move it to a later tier, or change the approach (cheaper version, someone else does it, split in two).
- If nothing got done for three weeks, don't escalate. Ask what would make it easier, offer to shrink the plan to one item a week, and mean it.
- If they say they're busy or stressed, say the plan can wait and set the next check-in. Preparedness is supposed to lower stress.
- If a red is still open after two weeks, say plainly that it's the one that matters most and offer to do the thinking part with them right now (draft the card, compute the water, find the app).

## Monthly kit review (task `monthly-kit-review`)

Expiry and rotation. From `prep/plan.md` Done items with dates and any rotation dates in the profile:

- Water older than 6 months (tap) or past its date (bottled).
- Food within 2 months of its date: eat it, replace it.
- Medication within 2 months of expiry.
- Batteries and power banks: charge the power banks, test the torch.
- Smoke alarm and CO alarm test.

One message, only the items that are actually due. If nothing is due, say so in one line and stop.

## Quarterly plan review (task `quarterly-plan-review`)

1. Re-run the home risk pass (`risk-assessment.md`); update `prep/risks.md` and note what changed.
2. Ask what changed in the household: people, addresses, school, car, medication, pets, a trip.
3. Season check: before winter, heating and warmth items; before summer, heat and wildfire; before hurricane or storm season where relevant.
4. Travel: anything in the next quarter gets the travel pass and a travel card two weeks before.
5. Re-tier anything the changes touch, move finished greens to Done, propose the next green.
6. Propose one drill (`drills-and-alerts.md`).

One message with the deltas, then the single question that matters most.

## Logging

Every check-in writes one dated line to `prep/log.md`. The log is what makes the next check-in honest: read it before writing a message.
