# Onboarding

Ten questions in five blocks. One block per message. Short answers are fine; say so. After each block, write what you learned to `prep/profile.md` (format in `additional_context/plan-format.md`) and confirm in one line what you noted, so they can correct it.

Don't ask for anything you can infer safely or look up (the official alert app for their country, the emergency numbers). Do ask for anything that changes quantities or the plan.

## Block 1: people and pets

1. Who lives with you? Names or roles, and an age band for each (infant, toddler, child, teen, adult, senior).
2. Any pets? Species and roughly how much they eat a day.

Write: People table, Pets table.

## Block 2: places

3. Where is home? Town and postcode are enough. Flat or house, which floor, is there a cellar, how is it heated, do you have a car?
4. Where else are you or the family regularly? Work, school, daycare, a parent's home. Town and postcode each.
5. Any trips planned in the next year? Where and when.

Write: Locations table, Travel table. If the `osm` server is present, geocode each location now (`references/nearby.md`) and store the coordinates in the profile.

## Block 3: needs

6. Does anyone depend on daily medication, medical devices, or mobility or sensory aids? You don't need to name the medication; "daily prescription, needs cooling" is enough. Include anyone with reduced mobility, hearing, sight, or who would need help leaving the building quickly.

Write: Medical and access needs. Then read `references/special-needs.md` before building the plan if anything is listed, and tell them you'll factor it in.

## Block 4: goal and money

7. How far do you want to go?
   - **basics**: get through 3 days at home without power, water or shops
   - **solid**: 10 days (what the German civil-protection office recommends)
   - **max**: 30+ days, plus power and communication that don't depend on the grid
8. What can you put into this? A one-off amount, a monthly amount, or "as little as possible". A €0 plan is a real plan; say so.
9. What do you already have? Free text. Count only what they say; don't assume.

Write: Goal, Budget, Already in place.

## Block 5: rhythm

10. When should I check in? A day and time each week works for most people. I'll keep it to a couple of lines.

Write: Check-in cadence. Then update the `weekly-check-in` task schedule and resume it; offer the monthly and quarterly tasks.

## After block 5

Say: "That's everything. Give me a moment to look up what's realistic for <home town> and <other places>." Then run `references/risk-assessment.md`, then `references/plan-builder.md`, and send the reds.

## If answers are partial

Build with what you have, mark the gap in the profile as `?`, and ask for it at the next check-in. Never block the plan on a missing answer except: people count, home location, goal.

## If they correct something later

Update the profile, re-run only the parts of the plan the change affects (a new baby changes water, food, go-bag and meds; a new postcode changes risks and alert settings), and say what moved.
