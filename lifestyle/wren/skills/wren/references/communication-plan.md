# Communication plan

Mirrors the ready.gov Family Emergency Communication Plan and wallet card. The point: the household may not be together when something happens, and networks may be down. Text, don't call; a text often gets through when a call won't.

## Collect

For each household member: name, mobile, email, school or workplace with address and its emergency pickup policy, any medical note that an emergency contact should know.

Then:

- **Out-of-area contact.** One person outside the region whom everyone texts "I'm OK, I'm at X". Local lines jam; long-distance often works. Tell that person they're it.
- **Meeting places.** Three: right outside home (for a fire), in the neighbourhood (if you can't go home), and out of town (if the area is evacuated). Make sure each is reachable for every household member, including anyone with a mobility aid. With the `osm` server, propose real candidates and give the foot time from home and from school or work (`references/nearby.md`).
- **Important numbers.** Local emergency number, doctor and paediatrician, pharmacy, vet, insurer, utility, school office, the official alert app's name, the municipal emergency meeting point if published.
- **"In Case of Emergency" contact** saved under that name in every phone, and the phone's medical ID filled in.

## Share

Write `prep/comms-card.md` so it fits on one printed page and folds to wallet size, in the household's language. One copy per person (wallet, school bag), one on the fridge, a photo on every phone. Children learn the out-of-area contact's number and the nearest meeting place by heart; practise it.

Format:

```
HOUSEHOLD EMERGENCY CARD                      updated <date>

Emergency: 112   Police: 110   Doctor on call: 116117
Alerts: NINA (home: Lindlar; also: Köln)

WE TEXT, WE DON'T CALL. Message: "OK at <place>".

Out-of-area contact: <name> <number>
Meeting 1 (outside home): <place>
Meeting 2 (neighbourhood): <place>
Meeting 3 (out of town): <place>

<name> <mobile> <school/work + pickup rule>
<name> <mobile> ...
Pets: <who takes them>

Doctor: … Paediatrician: … Pharmacy: … Vet: …
Insurance: … Utilities: …
```

## Practise

Put the first drill in amber: everyone texts the out-of-area contact from wherever they are; the family names the three meeting places from memory. Repeat at the quarterly review. Ask whether anyone's number, school or workplace has changed.
