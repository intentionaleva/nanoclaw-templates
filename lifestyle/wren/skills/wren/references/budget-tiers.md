# Budget tiers

Ask for the budget once (onboarding block 4). After that, every item carries a cost band and the plan never schedules more per week than the budget allows. Never quote a product price; prices change and the household's shops differ. Give the category and the band.

| Band | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| €0 | Nothing to buy |
| € | under 50 |
| €€ | 50 to 300 |
| €€€ | 300 to 1,500 |
| €€€€ | over 1,500 |

## What each budget buys, as a whole

**€0.** The communication card. Out-of-area contact. Meeting points. Alert app. Document photos backed up. Tap water in cleaned bottles and the empty containers you already own. One extra tin per shop. Handwritten contact list on the fridge. Knowing where the main water and gas shut-offs are. Smoke alarm test. Offline maps. A first drill at the kitchen table. This is a real plan and it covers the first 24 hours of most hazards.

**Around €50.** Bottled water to 3 days. Torch and batteries. A power bank. A first-aid refresh (plasters, bandages, thermometer, painkillers, rehydration salts). Bin liners, wet wipes, hand sanitiser. A small cash reserve. A battery radio if one isn't in the house.

**Around €500.** Water and food to 10 days. One go-bag per person. Hausapotheke to the full BBK list. A crank or solar radio. A second and larger power bank. A carbon-monoxide alarm. A fire-extinguishing spray. A camping stove with cartridges for outdoor use, or a stove rated for indoor use. Water containers for washing and flushing. A camping toilet if there's no cellar or garden.

**Around €5,000 and up.** A power station with solar panels sized to the devices that must run. An indoor-rated heater and its fuel, stored to the manufacturer's rule. A satellite messenger, or satellite internet where the risk pass found long outages or a remote location. A water filter rated for microbiological use. First-aid courses for the adults. Structural: shutters for heat, a prepared interior room with two walls between it and outside, a second full kit in the car. A fuel reserve only where legal and stored to the official rule.

## Rules

- A €0 red is never replaced by a bought item. The bought item is the amber or green upgrade.
- "Hardware first" is wrong. A household with a satellite dish and no water is less prepared than one with water and a paper contact list. Say this plainly if someone wants to start at the top.
- If the household names a hard ceiling, stay under it and say what green items wait on more room.
- If someone asks "is X worth it", answer with what it buys them for their risks, in one or two sentences, and where it sits in the plan. Don't sell.
