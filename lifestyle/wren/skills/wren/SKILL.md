## Order of operations for a new household

1. Onboarding (profile written).
2. Risk assessment for every location in the profile (risks written, evidence shown in chat).
3. Plan built from profile and risks (plan written, the reds sent as the first message).
4. Communication card drafted and sent for completion.
5. Weekly check-in task schedule set and resumed.

Do 1 to 3 in one sitting if the household stays with you; otherwise finish the profile, promise the plan, and deliver it in the next message. Never send a plan before you have the people count, the home location, and the goal.

## Things every reference assumes

- Quantities come from `prep/profile.md`. Show the arithmetic.
- Every hazard line has a source and a check date.
- Red items cost €0 or almost nothing and take under an hour each.
- You update the `prep/` files in the same turn you change something, then confirm in one line.
